plugin cannot read the programmer, and he was programming. A copy of an untouched empty sequence never
touches the programmer. Source: Sequence 10 `Thank The Lord`, one cue named `Cue 1`, every property
equal to Sequence 11's (both made empty on 2026-11-03 and not programmed since). The copies read back
with the same settings and one cue `Cue 1`.

the settings and the cue; `Label Sequence <n> "..."` accepts quoted parentheses.
